## 统一版式与材质

建议单张 1536×1024 横版，统一深蓝黑底、细蓝灰分隔线、象牙白标题、暖黄铜序号。信息层级为标题、3D 预览、六面视图、16×16 像素方案、色板和状态对照。文字可后期排版保证准确；不能用错误文字或伪像素网格冒充工程标注。

方块：左上占约 42% 的三分之四视角立体主图；右上为严格正投影的正/背/左/右/顶/底 2×3 视图；下部六张相对应的像素方案、状态差异和色板。六面要与主图一致。

物品：大幅像素主图、斜置薄片展示、16×16 网格方案、实际小尺寸可读性展示、色板；不虚构六面厚实体模型。

像素材质每个有效面按 16×16 设计，最多 1 像素深勾边，2～4 档手工分面明暗，大色块优先，不使用散乱噪点、连续渐变、照片纹理、写实金属反射、镜头景深或赛博霓虹。工程图文字可以平滑排版，但贴图像素只能以最近邻放大。

黄铜参考色直接取 Create 黄铜机壳：高光 #FFEB8C、亮面 #F7CB6C、中间调 #CEA05A、暖阴影 #9E6947、暗部 #724731。只在边框、角件、工具壳体使用，不能将整个机器染成金色。铝色为拟定设计色：#D8E1E4、#AEBEC8、#7D94A3、#4E6372。玻璃为稀疏 #C8E6E8 / #8DBFC8 高光加大片透明区域。共用深轮廓 #293941，红饰带 #8F3D35 / #C06B55。

工程图里的透明窗在深底或棋盘底上展示；这不代表导出的工程图整张透明。图像模型的概念图也不能自动视为经过像素校验的游戏 PNG。最终逐像素贴图、alpha、UV、连接纹理、模型和状态映射留下一轮确认制作。

### 01 航空箱 / Aviation Crate

Large clear glazing on all four vertical faces, narrow pale aluminum rim, small golden brass corner brackets and rivets. Opaque riveted aluminum top and bottom plates. The interior must be clearly visible, never fill the window with dark opaque blue. A restrained reflection is confined to the glass edge. Show one single-block isometric view and a small assembled 3-by-2 wall continuity demonstration: shared seams lose internal frames and corner hardware, leaving uninterrupted glass; structural framing remains around the external perimeter. Include a wrench-open state with the pane removed and a true opening while keeping the perimeter frame. Orthographic front/back/left/right are glazed, top/bottom are metal. Shared glass texture concept includes outer corner, outer edge and transparent center examples. Title: “航空箱 / AVIATION CRATE”.

### 02 交互舱口 / Interaction Hatch

Match the aviation crate aluminum family exactly. A clearly circular stepped pixel aperture centered in the front, a brass mechanical rim, a recessed dark interior, and a small brush plus milk-drop symbol readable at low resolution. Keep the actual aperture usable, not filled by an oversized emblem. Back, left, right, top and bottom are riveted aluminum panels. Three-quarter preview must face the operating aperture so the primary feature is visible. Show a small wall-mounted context thumbnail. This is a one-block interaction hatch, not the separate two-block rolling logistics door. Title: “交互舱口 / INTERACTION HATCH”.

### 03 回响激发器 / Echo Resonator

An exposed three-dimensional amethyst crystal cluster, using stepped faceted violet crystals of varied heights, above a dark sculk blue ring with sparse cyan branching veins. An andesite casing base with an evident shaft socket / mechanical transmission detail supports the ring. Reference the calibrated sculk sensor's crystal vocabulary; crystals protrude from the top and must not be merely painted on a cube side. Show identical geometry in inactive and active states, with only restrained cyan vein and amethyst highlight changes; no blurred glow that obscures pixel edges. Six orthographic silhouette views include the crystal geometry and the shaft base. Base plates and crystal facets have separate 16-by-16 texture design swatches. Registry reference: amethyst_sculk_resonator. Title: “回响激发器 / ECHO RESONATOR”.

### 04 生物打包机 / Creature Packager

Preserve the ivory body, deep red bands, status light, back connection motif and dark mechanical iris with overlapping spiral shutter segments. The front-facing isometric view must clearly show the iris. Show base idle and powered states with the same geometry and localized indicator differences. Add a small vertical-orientation thumbnail if space allows; linked-state design is reserved for a later pass. Six views consistently locate iris at front, connection motif at rear, red-band side panels, and matching top/bottom. Avoid recoloring red bands, white panels or iris blades gold. 1. 八项各有独立工程图，方块每张六面齐全且互相一致，物品不强加六面。
2. 3D 主图表现真实意图：透明大窗、立体水晶簇、可辨认虹膜、麻绳粮束。
3. 航空箱有连续玻璃与开窗；激发器、打包机有两态。
4. 只对照色板和几何确认概念，不把模型生成的大图裁块自动称作精确 16×16 游戏资源。
5. 后续制作像素源稿时单独校验尺寸、alpha、色数、最近邻缩放及游戏 UV，并以同一源稿重新渲染工程预览。
